## Description

Make YouTube searchable. TubeMind is your second brain for YouTube – free, no account, no tracking.

🔍 CTRL+F FOR VIDEOS
Press S and type a word. Every match in the transcript appears as a yellow marker on the progress bar. Press Enter to jump from match to match. Stop scrubbing for that one moment.

✨ FREE AI SUMMARIES – RIGHT ON YOUR DEVICE
TubeMind uses the AI built into Chrome (Gemini Nano). You get a short summary, key points and a timeline with clickable timestamps – no subscription, no server, nothing uploaded. On older computers TubeMind automatically shows the most important moments and topics without AI.

📝 TIMESTAMP NOTES
Press N at an important moment and start typing – the video pauses automatically. B sets a quick bookmark. Notes appear as markers in the video and export as Markdown to Notion, Obsidian, Logseq and more.

📚 YOUR PERSONAL LIBRARY
All videos with notes and summaries in one place. Even search the saved transcripts: "Where did someone say that again?"

📄 TRANSCRIPT IN THE SIDE PANEL
Read along, click a line to jump there, copy or download the transcript.

🧘 FOCUS MODE
Hide Shorts, the home feed, recommendations, comments and end screens. Optionally open Shorts in the normal player. Set a daily budget and see how much time you really spend on YouTube.

⚡ PLAYER COMFORT
• Remembers your speed per channel (e.g. podcasts at 1.5×, tutorials at 1×)
• Speeds up to 16× (adjustable) with A / D, R for normal
• Automatic theater mode and preferred quality

🔒 PRIVATE AND FAIR
• All data stays on your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking.
• No ads on YouTube. TubeMind shows one small, clearly marked sponsor tip inside its own panel – that keeps it free.
• Every feature can be switched off.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
S – search in video · N – note · B – bookmark · A/D – slower/faster · R – normal speed
